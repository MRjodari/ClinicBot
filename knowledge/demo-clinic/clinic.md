## How treatment works for international patients
1. Send a recent panoramic x-ray (taken within the last 6 months) to the coordinator by WhatsApp or email, if you have one.
2. The dentist reviews it and the coordinator sends a preliminary plan and price range, usually within 2 working days. This is free and not binding.
3. First visit in İzmir: examination, 3D scan (CBCT) if needed, final treatment plan and final price. The examination and scan are free if you go ahead with treatment.
4. Treatment. The coordinator sends a day-by-day schedule before you book flights.

## Typical time in İzmir
- Veneers or zirconia crowns: 5–7 working days, one trip.
- Implants: two trips. Trip 1: implant placement, 3–4 days. Trip 2 after 3–4 months of healing: crowns, 5–7 days.
- Teeth whitening: 1 day.
- Fillings, root canal, cleaning: 1–3 days.

## Payment
- Cash (EUR, GBP, USD, TL), credit card, bank transfer.
- 10% deposit to confirm treatment dates; the rest is paid at the clinic. Deposit is refundable up to 7 days before arrival.

## Guarantees and aftercare
- Implants: 10-year manufacturer guarantee on the implant; 5-year clinic guarantee on crowns and veneers.
- Free check-up if you return within the guarantee period; the coordinator stays in touch by WhatsApp after you go home.

## Frequently asked questions
- Is it painful? Treatments are done under local anaesthesia; sedation is available for anxious patients at extra cost (ask the coordinator).
- Can I fly after implant surgery? Usually yes, after 24–48 hours; the dentist confirms for each patient.
- Do you treat children? Yes, the clinic has a paediatric dentist on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- Do I need a visa? It depends on your nationality; check the Turkish e-visa site. The clinic does not arrange visas.
- Can I get an invoice for my health insurance? Yes, an official invoice and a treatment report in English or German are provided.
